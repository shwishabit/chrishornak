Dark-first. Teal is the signal accent — the dot, links, key emphasis. Never pure black (#000), never a teal background (the dot would disappear).

Typography

The wordmark is custom lettering — always use the provided vector, don't retype it in a system font. On-site typography: Sora (headings) + Inter (body).

Usage

Do

  • Keep the teal dot on every use — it's the signature.
  • Dark-first: off-white on near-black is the default.
  • Use teal for the dot, links, and single points of emphasis.
  • Give the mark clear space of at least half its height.

Don't

  • Don't put the mark on a teal background — the dot vanishes.
  • Don't recolor or drop the teal dot.
  • Don't use pure black (#000) or generic startup blue.
  • Don't set the wordmark in any face but Sora 700.
