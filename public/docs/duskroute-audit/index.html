Duplicate data is part of it, but not the main cause. Three settings hide the site's best pages from Google. Fix those first.

What I found

Most serious first
1

CriticalVenue pages are set to noindex.

websters-bar-newtown and lord-dudley-hotel both carry noindex, follow. These are your most unique pages, so Google judges the site on templates.

Fix

Remove the noindex from venue pages and add them to the sitemap.

2

Criticalrobots.txt blocks the CSS and JavaScript.

Disallow: /_next/static/chunks/ blocks the files every page needs to render.

Fix

Delete that line. Check the screenshot in URL Inspection > View tested page.

3

CriticalEach suburb has up to 4 pages of the same data.

595 template pages: suburb, /happyhour, /statistics, /drink-prices. The 252 /happyhour pages sit in 2 sitemaps outside the index, blocked in robots.txt, yet live.

Fix

301 /happyhour to the suburb page. Merge stats and prices into it, or noindex them under ~10 venues.

4

MinorEvery sitemap date is the same.

Almost every URL shows 2026-10-07T02:46, so Google ignores lastmod.

Fix

Use the date the deals on each page changed.

5

NextVery little content only Dusk Route has.

Most pages are templates. You already have the data for pages no one else can publish.

Fix

A monthly price report ("What a pint costs in Sydney"), human-written suburb intros, venue photos.

6

NextAlmost no real links, and a pile of spam ones.

DR 1.6. Nearly all 286 linking sites are one .shop spam network. Google ignores most of these.

Fix

Pitch the price report to Time Out and Broadsheet. Offer venues a badge. Disavow only after a manual action.

What to noindex, and what to keep

Never block a noindexed page in robots.txt

Noindex

  • Stats and prices pages under ~10 venues
  • Suburbs with only 1 or 2 venues
  • /venue-search, /nearby, and /map unless it gets traffic

Keep indexed

  • Venue pages, suburbs with real depth, region pages
  • List pages such as /sydney/cheapest-pints
  • About, contact, privacy and terms

Start in Search Console > Manual actions. A penalty there needs a reconsideration request after the fixes. No penalty means make the fixes, then request indexing for your 10 to 20 best pages.
