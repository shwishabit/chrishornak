What gets built

Not in this fee
  • New photography or the hero graphic. I will spec what is needed and place whatever you supply. See decision 5.
  • Copywriting from scratch. I will tighten and fit what exists; net-new sections are a separate line.
  • The 5-stick trial SKU and the Day Zero nurture flow from the August audit. Still the right move — but it touches fulfilment and email, and it is not a two-week decision. Phase 2, priced separately.
  • Other templates. Cart, collection, account. PDP only.
  • App subscriptions or fulfilment changes.

Leave alone

The cost comparison table and The Science of Synergy are the best-built things you own. Neither is being touched beyond the number sweep.

Decisions, fee, and what I need

Eight decisions still open.

Seven are yours, from your own To finalize list. The eighth is mine. None of them block the start — all of them block the finish, so the sooner they land the more room the last week has.

1

Two testimonials are still placeholder.

Card 1 (Jennifer B.) is real. Cards 2 and 3 need real quotes, names and photos. Misty M. and Brittany G-E. were flagged as candidates in your Junip export — I can pull both if you approve them.

2

Which bullet leads — creatine or clean label?

Also the final wording of the taste line and nothing else in it. I need the five bullets locked as a list I can paste.

3

The study footnote has no footnote.

89% / 86% carries a 1 with nothing under it. Needs one line of base disclosure — participants and the logging threshold. A claim with a visible base reads stronger than one without.

4

Kit value: $85, and the live page says $139.

The live badge counts the $89.99 supply itself as a free gift, and still lists the $5 tracker you dropped. Confirm $85 and I correct it everywhere in one pass.

5

The hero image has text baked into it.

“$2.31 per day” and “$139 Value” are inside the picture, they disagree with the new page, and they are unreadable on a phone. Cleanest answer is a re-export with no text. If that is not available by the 22nd, I will crop and set the claims in live type instead.

6

Confirm the ticker comes out.

It says the same thing as the testimonial row, one screen apart. Your notes recommend cutting it; I need that as a yes.

7

Access, on day one.

Shopify collaborator access with theme permissions, plus whoever owns the Junip and subscription apps on a thread I can reach. This is the one item that can stall everything behind it.

8

When does stock actually return?

Mine. The page needs a real date in the banner, and the launch should be timed to it. Give me the date you would defend, not the optimistic one.
