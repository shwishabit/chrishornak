The Opportunity

Capital isn't reaching where Africa's energy sector needs it.

Public and development finance for African energy has fallen roughly a third in a decade, even as private clean-energy investment nearly triples. The shortfall isn't a lack of capital – it's the bridge between global investors and African execution.

Source: IEA, World Energy Investment 2025 & Financing Electricity Access in Africa
