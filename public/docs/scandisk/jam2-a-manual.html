SCANDISK · BAD SECTOR

Jam 2 · TEAM A (tactics) · play it

Round 4. Title → STORY (3 lines, auto) → three lines and the win → the drive plays a board for you and says what it is doing → the same board, your turn. All six rules are on this page, not in your way in the game. The drive is rotting under your feet. You cannot shoot. You can only shove the virus into the drive's own holes — and you have to crash every last red before the clock runs out.

WIN: CRASH EVERY RED BEFORE THE BACKUP BAR FILLS. The bar is your deadline, not your prize. Fill it with red still alive and the board simply ends — SURVIVED. Crash them all and it is PURGED. Only a PURGE takes you to the next sector. (round 4 · A1)

THE SIX RULES

  1. Two PROGRAMS. Each gets 3 steps a round. A step walks one tile, or SHOVES what is next to it one tile.
  2. A red shoved into a wall, a hole, a file or another red CRASHES. Shoving is your only weapon.
  3. Every red shows its next hit one round early. It all happens when you press START.
  4. WORM walks at the nearest file and eats it. SPIKE fires down its line, kills the first thing in it, then turns a quarter clockwise.
  5. The drive rots where you killed. The tile a red died on last round CRACKS. At the end of this round it is a hole — and whatever stands on it crashes. (round 4 · A3 — it used to be a die roll over every open tile)
  6. Crash every red and the sector is PURGED — that is the win. Lose a FILE, or both programs, and it is LOST. Fill the TIME bar with red still alive and the board just ends: SURVIVED. (round 4 · A1)

BUTTONS

  • Arrows / D-pad — the whole game. Walk, or shove. There is no attack button.
  • X — swap to your other program. Z — undo a step (as far back as the start of the round).
  • START — end the round. Only then does the virus move.
  • MANUAL / Backspace — this page.

WHAT YOU SEE

THE TUTORIAL THAT PLAYS ITSELF (team call, round 2)

Round 1 gave you a gym and asked you to solve it cold. Chris cut that. Now:

  1. Before move one it names the goal: WIN: CRASH EVERY RED BEFORE THE BACKUP BAR FILLS.
  2. The bot plays a hand-built board. Nothing random, no cracking tile — just the virus and you.
  3. Its first move on screen is always the money move: SHOVED IT INTO A HOLE. GONE. The board has no dice in it, so that is a guarantee, not a hope.
  4. Every step it takes, a short line names the move and the reason. One idea per line.
  5. The top of the screen always reads WATCHING — ANY BUTTON = YOUR TURN.
  6. Press anything and you get the board from the top. Every button does the same thing — there is no separate skip, and nothing on screen calls it one. (Thresh fix: taking over early used to hand you the half-played board after the bot had made the good moves.)
  7. When it finishes, you get the same board from the start, now that you know the ask.

The board teaches four things in order: a worm with a hole behind it (one shove), a spike aimed at MOM.WAV with a hole under it (a file dies on START unless you move it), a worm against the right edge (the edge is a wall too), and the TIME bar counting down.

SEE IT BEFORE YOU DO IT (team call, round 2)

Into the Breach's rule, borrowed whole: you may know the exact result of a move before you commit. For the program you are holding, every red next to you is already answered on the board.

  • A white X on a red — shove that way and it CRASHES. No maths, no counting.
  • A hollow red box one tile further — that is where it lands instead. It does not die.
  • A red pip on a file — that is the file a WORM is walking at. The HUD used to say this in words (DANGER: n OF YOURS DIES ON START); the row is gone and the warning now sits on the object. (round 4 · Thresh call)

TELLING THE TWO REDS APART (team call, round 2)

  • WORM — the heaviest thing on the board. A full solid tile with a dark bite out of it. It is the one that eats your files, so it is the one that shouts.
  • SPIKE — a hollow shell with a bright barrel. A machine, not a mouth. It is dangerous but it tells you where it is pointing, so it does not need the weight.

The BLOCKED nag was deleted to pay for it: the board now shows you what is legal, so it does not need to tell you off.

THE PAD

THREE THINGS TO TRY

  1. Shove a worm into a SPIKE's dotted line. The virus shoots its own unit.
  2. Kill a red on a tile you want to keep and watch what happens: the drive rots where you killed, so that tile is next round's crack. Where you crash something is now a decision. (round 4 · A3)
  3. Shove a red onto the CRACK and leave it there. The drive kills it for free at the end of the round.
  4. When a spike's shot frames one of your files, do not walk into the line — shove a red into it instead.

CALLS WE MADE FOR YOU (team calls)

  • Losing ONE file loses the sector. Three files means three things to guard, not three lives.
  • Spikes turn clockwise after every shot, so you can read them rounds ahead.
  • You cannot shove your own program, and you cannot walk into a hole.
  • A program out of steps hands over to the other one by itself.
  • Holes and beams: a beam flies over a hole, so a hole is cover for nothing.
  • The crack is always shown before it opens. Nothing in this game is a surprise.
  • The story: the brief gave us "the last machine, a virus eating the drive". The rotting platter that drops tiles away under you is our line.
  • Numbers: 8x6 drive, 3 steps each, 5 reds, clock 8 rounds. All in src/jam2a/tune2a.ts.
  • SURVIVED is not a loss and does not get the win sound — the same quiet four notes, one lower. (Thresh call)
  • Only a PURGE takes you deeper. SURVIVED re-rolls the same sector. (team call)
  • If no red died last round — or the tile it died on is no longer open — the crack falls back to the old random pick. Nothing else does. (team call)
