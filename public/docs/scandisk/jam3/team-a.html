  1. Trap virus chips in a line to flip them, like Othello. Pick your chip's type first.
  2. SYS beats EXE, EXE beats DAT, DAT beats SYS. A line holding a chip that beats yours is GUARDED and won't flip.
  3. Flipped chips keep their type. Each one yours beats RESCANS: it fires its own lines. Chains carry on.
  4. You can't place the same type twice in a row.
  5. No flip? QUARANTINE: place any type next to your own chip. Nothing flips.
  6. Ends when the disk is full or a side can't place. Most chips wins.
