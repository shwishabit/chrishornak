Instruction manual · test build v9 QUARANTINE · 2026-09-18

It is growing. You can see exactly where. You have two stones to cut it off.

The rules in one minute

  1. Your turn: place 2 blue stones on empty points.
  2. Then the virus grows: every red group grows 1 cell onto the spot it marked (red corners). A blue stone on a mark blocks it.
  3. Mood: the top line shows the mood now and the NEXT one. HUNT = its marks go toward your stones. SPREAD = toward open space. BURROW = toward the nearest file.
  4. Surround a red group on every side and it is deleted. If red surrounds a blue group, it is infected and turns red.
  5. 3 blue in a straight line = FIREWALL (a bright bar). Its group is never infected, and red can't grow onto a point next to it (the small blue crosses).
  6. Delete the group that holds the ROOT (the red cell with the bright core): every red cell crashes, and the sector is clean. If red grows onto a file, the file is lost. Lose all 3 files and the sector is lost.

A file is not a free side: it can't be used to breathe, and you can't place a stone on it. Guard it from the sides.

Buttons

One short lesson comes first: SURROUND THE ROOT.

The hints

  • Blue dot: a stone here deletes a red group.
  • Hollow green box: a stone here makes a new firewall.
  • Red frame around your stones: the growth now on the board would infect them.
  • Grey corners: a mark you blocked.

How a sector ends

In bot tests (a simple greedy player, 100 boards), the root dies by round 15 in about 7 of 10 boards; a board takes about 8 rounds.
