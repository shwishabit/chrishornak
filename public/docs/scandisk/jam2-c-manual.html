SCANDISK · PURGE

Jam 2 · TEAM C · action puzzle · 2026-09-19

The virus is eating the drive. Plant charges, set one off, and blow a whole sector clean in one chain.

The story

Start goes to this screen first. Five lines, then the rules, then the demo plays itself.

The six rules

  1. Each beat the red virus grows onto its mark (the red corners). It marks more cells the longer a sector runs.
  2. X on an empty cell: plant a charge. You have 8 in all, and that is the whole supply. A charge on a mark blocks that growth.
  3. X on a charge: it goes off and comes straight back to your hand. A beam shoots out four ways.
  4. A beam stops at the first cell it meets. Red dies. Your blue data dies. It runs through your own charges and sets them off on the way.
  5. Charges that go off together are your CHAIN. Red killed × chain = score.
  6. No red left: sector clean. No data left: drive lost. Clear all 7 sectors: drive saved.

Buttons

The pad under the screen works the same on a PC and on a phone. On a PC it lights up with your keys.

XPlant a charge, or set off the charge you are standing on. After a run: new run.

Reading the screen

  • Top bar: the beat filling. When it is full, the virus grows.
  • Line 1: sector · red left · data left · score.
  • Line 2: what X does here. On a charge it reads the whole blast before you press it: CHAIN 4 KILL 9 -1 DATA. The blue pips on the right are the charges still in your hand, out of eight.
  • On the board: red corners = where the virus grows next beat. A thin blue line between two charges = they can see each other, so they chain. A white box = a red cell this blast kills. A red cross = your own data this blast burns.

The tutorial plays itself

There is no gym to fumble through any more. The same bot that proves the game in the sim plays the teaching board on screen, slowly, and says one plain line about each move as it makes it: why it planted there, why these two line up, what a beat costs it.

Press any button at any time and it stops and says YOUR TURN. Press Enter to skip the whole thing. When the demo ends you get the same board to play yourself, now that you have seen what it was asking for.

The demo drives its own clock. The beat only moves when the demo says so, so the words never fall behind the game. (team call)

The trade-off

Waiting makes the chain bigger. Waiting also lets the virus grow between your charges, and a red cell in the gap cuts the link. Your own beams burn your own data, so the biggest blast is often not the right one.

Calls the team made for Chris (say if any are wrong)

  • A blast is worked out against the board as it was when you pressed X, so the preview is always exactly right.
  • The beat holds while a chain is rippling, so a big one is never cut short.
  • Z takes a charge back and refunds it (phone mis-taps).
  • When the virus has no room to grow beside itself, it drops new infections anywhere on the drive, including on a data cell. That is what stops a walled-in stall, and it is why no run runs for ever.
  • Firing re-aims the virus's marks. Setting off a small charge to move a mark off your data is a real tactic, and it costs you a charge.
  • Charges are conserved. Eight, full stop — hand plus board always adds up to eight, and one comes back the moment it goes off. This is round 2's one rule change. Before, a charge came back every beat, so anyone who just spammed charges built a 25-charge blob that chained huge by accident: the random bot out-chained the smart bot 9.8 to 6.0. Now the board can only ever hold eight, so a big chain has to be arranged. Smart 5.6, random 3.3.
  • There is no gym. The bot demos the game and then hands you the same board.
  • 7 sectors per run. The beat is 4.8s falling to 4.2s (round 2 clawed it back from 5.6s, which was only ever slack bought to make the game winnable).
