Wipe out the virus. Flip its stones.

1The rules in one minute

  1. You are blue. The virus is red. The board is 6×6. You each place one stone per turn. Stones never move.
  2. A new stone must touch one of your stones.
  3. The flipper (every other turn): put it next to a red stone, and that stone turns blue.
  4. Surround a red group on all sides and it is removed (like Go).
  5. 3 in a row makes a tower. Towers that see or touch each other link into a fence. Towers and fences can't be flipped.
  6. Towers are life: lose your last tower, and you have 2 rounds to build one. If you don't, your stones crash.
  7. You win when the virus has no stones, or no place left to put one.

There are no points and no clock. The bar under the top line shows the stones on the board: blue from the left, red from the right. All blue = you won.

Four practice boards come first: TAKE, BUILD, FLIP, then READ THE VIRUS, which becomes your first real board.

3The start: read the virus

The virus marks 4 spots and has secretly chosen 2. The marks are off-limits: place your 2 stones near where it might land. One mark then fades: it was a bluff. You may move one stone (X pick up, X drop, or Z Z to keep). Then the virus lands.

→

4Touch: grow from your stones

A plain stone must touch one of your stones (up, down, left or right). The cursor turns grey where you can't place. Your flipper is the one way to reach further.

5The flipper

The two small chips at the top right are the flippers: yours, then the virus's. A solid chip = ready this turn. A hollow chip with a number = the turns left until it is ready. You get a flipper every other turn, and so does the virus.

  1. Press Z. The cursor becomes the flipper, and every red stone you can flip gets blue brackets.
  2. Move next to a red stone and press X. It turns blue.
  3. If that point touches two red stones, press the arrow toward the one you want, then X.
→
A flip can set off more: the flipped stone can finish a surround (a capture) or 3 in a row (a tower).

6Capture (like Go)

Stones that touch form a group. Fill every empty point next to a group and it is removed.

→
You may not place where your own group would have no empty point, unless it captures. A board position can never repeat (the Go ko rule).

7Towers and fences

Put 3 stones in a straight line, then pick the one that becomes the tower (X). The other two go. Z Z keeps your wall instead.

Two towers that see each other (a straight line, only empty points between) link into a fence. New in v8: towers that touch link too. A fence and its towers are one group, and they can't be flipped.

Steal: put a stone just past both ends of a red fence (the small boxes), and the fence becomes yours. A closed loop of fences can't be stolen.

8How a board ends

  • Wiped out: the virus has no stones left.
  • Contained: it is the virus's turn and it has no legal place for a stone. It works the same way for you, so keep room to grow.
  • Crashed: a side that has built towers and then loses its last one has 2 rounds to build another.
  • Stalemate: both sides pass in a row. Nobody wins.

In bot tests, most boards end by round 40 (median about 34 rounds).
