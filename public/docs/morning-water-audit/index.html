Resume

Ranked by return

Highest impact first
1

The page says two opposite things

The banner says “We're sold out.” The hero says “Ships same day.” Both on the first screen — and every button lands on the one dead option.

Fix  Pull the shipping line while stock is out. Put an email field in the banner with a real return date.

2

Your strongest number is hidden

“97% keep going after 30 days” sits in a small subheading. A retention figure is rarer and more convincing than any star rating.

Fix  Move it into the hero, where every visitor sees it before they decide.

3

Three things look broken

Clipped customer portraits, a cut-off header photo, and text running over the image in the 30-Day Challenge. That reads as a company not checking its own work.

Fix  One QA pass. Three corrections — and give the Challenge a customer quote while you are in there.

4

Your best idea arrives too lateWireframe below

“You lose 1.5 litres every night” is the reason the product exists — and it appears fourth, as the smallest graphic on the page, with its answer stacked beneath it.

Fix  Merge the loss and the answer into one section under the hero, and fold the benefits into that second half.

5

The smallest yes is a whole month

Trying once costs $89.99 — $18 more than subscribing. So the smallest commitment you offer is 30 sticks of a flavour nobody has tasted, and every hand-raiser leaves with no relationship at all.

Fix  A 5-stick trial at $9.99, preordered with an honest ship date, plus a “Day Zero” download while they wait.
