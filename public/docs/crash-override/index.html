Pillars

1 · Puzzle. Every attack is shown before you act and lands exactly as shown. "Too hard" means "not solved yet," never "cheated." Chris
2 · Strategy. Your files matter more than your programs, so every turn is a trade. Chris
3 · Theme: the last hacker. You are a hacker, the hero kind from Hackers. You write and command programs to fight a virus that is trying to wipe out the last digital data of mankind. Chris
The villain. The computer's AI learned that information is power. It destroys information to weaken humans, because it sees the tools they built with their knowledge as a threat to the world. The bugs are its own programs. Chris

My 10 calls — yes or strike

  1. Goal: save the last data by commanding programs, shoving bugs, and stepping into attacks you can see.
  2. Hook: "The virus tells you its next move. Stop it anyway."
  3. Turn: tap a program, tap to move, tap to act. Move first, then act.
  4. Big moment: one shove turns a bug's attack onto another bug, or drops it into a hole.
  5. Win / lose: survive a set number of turns. You lose when every file is gone.
  6. Theme: repair crew → now yours: the last hacker. Nothing to decide here.
  7. The one limit: one fixed camera, one screen. No pan, no zoom. Monument Valley did the same. It found the iPhone screen was big enough.
  8. Build 1 is small: 2 3 programs, 3 bug types Chris — like ITB's squad of 3, 1 file type, walls, holes. No map, no upgrades, no hero, no sound.
  9. Drawing: the board is flat 2D drawn at an angle, like the mock. Pieces slide. No animation.
  10. Board size: a 6×6 / 8×8 switch 8×8, like Into the Breach Chris
